## B06/F01: reset and retention decisions

The [reset preflight](../../../scripts/maintenance/reset_supabase_data.sql#L38) joins `pg_trigger`, `pg_class` and `pg_namespace` and rejects `public` triggers where `(tgtype & 32) <> 0` and `NOT tgisinternal`. Its [C046 helper](SECURITY_CONTAINMENT_MIGRATION_CANDIDATE.sql#L312) always raises SQLSTATE `42501`. Therefore, **if C048 is installed, the existing reset script refuses before its destructive operation**, even for an empty audit table and without needing to invoke the new helper.

Merely adding the audit table to that array would not address the preceding schema-wide trigger rejection. No reset, trigger, ACL, retention or application changes were made.
