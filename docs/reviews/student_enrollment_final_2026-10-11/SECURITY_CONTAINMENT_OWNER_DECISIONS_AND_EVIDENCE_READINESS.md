### N01 — backend time used for claim eligibility

**OPEN. Medium potential expiry/order risk; high confidence in source, delay/skew outcome untested.** [Institution claim:220](../../../backend/app/repositories/platform_admin_invitations.py#L220) has a DB-time claim in its docstring at 226–230, but [232–245](../../../backend/app/repositories/platform_admin_invitations.py#L232) builds a Python timestamp before execution and uses it for `accepted_at` and `expires_at > supplied_time`. [Super-admin claim:228–240](../../../backend/app/services/super_admin_registration.py#L228) likewise supplies backend timestamps, including separate computations at 234 and 239. A conditional table update is not evidence that its time bound was captured at the locked DB decision.

Proposed options: (a) minimal DB-owned expiry/accepted-time decision combined with current digest/generation comparison; this does not solve pre-claim provider effects or cross-system recovery; (b) preferred P01 durable reservation with current digest/generation and one DB wall-clock decision timestamp captured after lock acquisition. OD03 must accept strict boundary eligibility and OD04 the separate completion deadline. The design cites `clock_timestamp()` rather than transaction-start `now()` for a post-wait decision; target-version behavior requires E10/E13 and later validation. Changing a comment or substituting a backend timestamp does not repair the contract.

Compatibility: exact-expiry, queued claims, clock-skew cases and `accepted_at` meaning can change; both invitation kinds and every mutator must share the chosen contract. Existing links/backfill and administrative sweeps need OD18 acceptance. Approval dependencies: OD03/OD04/OD10/OD18/OD20; E01/E10–E13/E21/E24–E26; P01/P03/P06; B01/B04/B05/B06/B07. **T11/T12/T14 NOT RUN**; D07/D08 cover exact expiry, lock-wait crossing, backend skew and claimed-before-expiry completion. T04 also covers atomic reservation/event failure. Observations must include locked decision time, commit ordering, rows/events and zero provider I/O on denied begin.

### N02 — student approval calls an incompatible role repository

**OPEN. Medium confirmed source compatibility defect; high confidence, no application invocation.** [Service approval write:469](../../../backend/app/services/admin_academics.py#L469) precedes [role call:486–492](../../../backend/app/services/admin_academics.py#L486), which omits required [keyword actor:805–812](../../../backend/app/repositories/tenancy.py#L805). [Wrapper:835–836](../../../backend/app/repositories/tenancy.py#L835) permits staff/faculty only, so adding an actor alone still cannot grant student through that wrapper. [Catch:493–502](../../../backend/app/services/admin_academics.py#L493) logs the role failure and returns the approval; [route:928–939](../../../backend/app/api/admin.py#L928) then records a student-approval audit. This is a source-broken branch, not a successful student SQL entry or observed runtime failure.

Proposed options: (a) preferred separately reviewed student command deriving the verified approver and stored tenant/target, atomically deciding approval, granting the student role and writing required audit; (b) if product intentionally separates approval from provisioning, approve a durable role-provisioning intent/result and explicit pending/failure response rather than best-effort implied grant; (c) retire the role call only if product explicitly revises the documented approval-grants-role contract and supplies a legitimate replacement path. Simply supplying the missing actor, treating the student as its own approver or widening staff/faculty/invitation allowlists is insufficient.

Compatibility: current service signature lacks actor provenance; endpoint/service/repository and existing institution/platform-admin modalities need a deliberate transition. Define handling of previously approved users lacking roles through separately approved, non-destructive reconciliation; do not backfill here. Student lifecycle triggers, roster/visibility behavior and subject-as-actor ambiguity B05-F09 remain separate obligations.

Approval dependencies: OD06/OD07/OD11/OD14/OD18/OD19/OD20; E02/E11–E15/E18/E21/E25/E26; P03/P05/P06; B01/B02/B04/B05/B06/B07. **T04/T09/T11/T12 NOT RUN**; T10 retains student/legacy projections and T14 covers atomic failure/races if a successor is scoped. No unrelated invitation role expansion is proposed.

### N03 — email confirmation precedes guarded approval arbitration

**OPEN. Medium potential external side-effect/order risk; high source confidence, provider outcome untested.** [Provider confirmation helper:78–89](../../../backend/app/services/admin_memberships.py#L78) invokes Auth admin `email_confirm`. [Pending approval:149–157](../../../backend/app/services/admin_memberships.py#L149) calls it before the role/decision RPC. [SQL lock/state decision:25–39](../../../supabase/migrations/20261009000000_phase_7_24_immediate_staff_faculty_approval.sql#L25) and [actor assertion:58–60](../../../supabase/migrations/20261009000000_phase_7_24_immediate_staff_faculty_approval.sql#L58) occur later. [Already-applied service path:132–140](../../../backend/app/services/admin_memberships.py#L132) also confirms email; [SQL:34–35](../../../supabase/migrations/20261009000000_phase_7_24_immediate_staff_faculty_approval.sql#L34) returns already-applied before the actor assertion. These are recorded source sequences, not new unauthenticated HTTP invocation or ordinary-client disclosure findings.

Proposed options: (a) durable authorized approval reservation/intent before provider I/O, then guarded request/role finalization after known confirmation; or (b) guarded atomic immediate request/role/audit approval plus durable confirmation task/result, with sign-in readiness pending until established. Option (a) can delay role availability; option (b) permits role completion before sign-in readiness and needs accepted baseline-access and response semantics. Both require authorization before idempotent disclosure, one-operation retry/result evidence, reconciliation of unknown provider outcomes and no database transaction held across provider calls. Merely moving the call after SQL without durable intent/result leaves a recovery gap.

Compatibility: preserve intentional immediate staff/faculty approval, not invitation issuance or a password requirement. Coordinate existing provider-confirmed/DB-pending and role-approved/email-pending states without declaring rollback of confirmation. Owners must decide API/UI readiness and reject-versus-approve ordering. Approval dependencies: OD07/OD12/OD13/OD14/OD18/OD20; E02/E10–E13/E16–E19/E25/E26; P03/P05/P06; B01/B04/B05/B06/B07. **T04/T11/T12/T14 NOT RUN**; D24 covers rejection/authorization/audit failure before confirmation, confirmation definitive failure/timeout, lost responses, replay and independent request/role/audit/provider observations.
