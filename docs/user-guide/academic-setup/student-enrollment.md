# Step 5 — Student enrollment

Enrollment places a student in a section. The student then registers for that
semester's subjects from their own portal.

[← Batches and sections](batches-and-sections.md) · Next: [Rules and troubleshooting →](rules-and-troubleshooting.md)

## Before you start

- The student has an **approved, active** account in your institution. Inviting
  and approving students is covered in the student management guide.
- The section exists and its section subjects are set up (see
  [Batches and sections](batches-and-sections.md)).

## 5.1 Assign a student to a section (administrator)

1. Open **Academic Sections** and select **Manage Student Enrollment**.
2. In **Student Enrollment**, choose the section, for example
   `CSE26-A-S1 — Section A (Semester 1)`.
3. Choose the student under **Select an approved student**.
4. Select **Assign student**.

The student appears under **Semester assignments** with the status **Awaiting
subject selection**.

## 5.2 Register subjects (student)

1. The student signs in and opens **Semester Registration**.
2. They see the **Common required subjects** for their section, which they take
   automatically.
3. For each choice group, they pick **exactly one** option.
4. They select **Register selected subjects**.

The page shows **Registration complete**, and in **Student Enrollment** the
status changes to **Registered**.

## Example: Greenfield College

| Who | Action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | Assigns *Asha Rao* to `CSE26-A-S1` | Asha shows as *Awaiting subject selection* |
| Asha | Opens Semester Registration, sees Programming Fundamentals and Engineering Mathematics I, selects **Register selected subjects** | *Registration complete* |
| Administrator | Reviews **Semester assignments** | Asha shows as *Registered* |

## Common problems

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The student is not in the list | The account is not approved or not active. | Approve or reactivate the student first. |
| The student sees **Subject unavailable** | A compulsory or choice subject is not available to the section. | Add it in **Subjects by section**. |
| "The section or a selected subject has reached capacity" | The section or a section subject is full. | Raise the capacity (within the section's capacity) or use another section. |
